Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about blood donation.

1. What is blood donation?

Blood donation is the process of giving blood to help patients who need blood for medical treatment.

2. Who can donate blood?

A healthy person who meets the required age, weight, and medical conditions may be able to donate blood. A medical professional should confirm eligibility.

3. How can I find a blood donor?

Open the Find Donor page, select the required blood group, and enter your city to search for donors.

4. How can I request blood in an emergency?

Open the Emergency page and enter the patient's information and blood requirements.

5. Can I update my donor profile?

Yes. You can open your Profile page to view your information. Profile editing will be connected through the backend.

6. Is this website a replacement for hospitals?

No. This website helps users find donors and hospitals. In a medical emergency, contact emergency services or a healthcare professional.
